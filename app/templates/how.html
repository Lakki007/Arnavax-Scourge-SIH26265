How Scourge worksThe bank's server is the witness.

Today an FDR is confirmed by a letter to the branch, so most deposits are checked once in ten years. Scourge lets the account holder prove what the bank's own server says about the deposit, revealing only the deposit fields.

{{ lanes_static() }}
  1. AICTE asks. An officer requests a proof for one FDR. The challenge is single use, bound to that institution, and expires in 15 minutes.
  2. The institution proves. From its own net-banking session it fetches the deposit page inside a TLS session whose keys are shared with AICTE's verifier (TLSNotary MPC-TLS).
  3. The verifier checks. Certificate chain, an approved bank host and endpoint, HTTP 200 with data, one deposit record matching the FD number. Everything else stays hidden.
  4. AICTE decides. The app compares the proven fields with the register: verified, discrepancy, insufficient evidence or rejected. Release needs an accepted confirmation.

A five-minute walkthrough

  1. Sign in as the AICTE officer. Open FDR-001 and choose Request bank proof.
  2. Choose Switch to INST-42 to answer it, then Prove from my bank. Watch the real stages arrive.
  3. Open the evidence: the register beside the bank's answer, and the exact bytes AICTE received, with everything else blacked out. Accept it as the officer, then release.
  4. In Mock bank, set FD-0000777777 to "Loan raised against the deposit", then prove it with the Accounts summary page: discrepancy. The PAN and other accounts stay hidden.
  5. Under "Try to cheat", connect to your own fake bank: rejected on the certificate.
  6. In Baseline, upload samples/edited_receipt_FD-0000777777.png for the same deposit: the baseline approves it, while the bank's server contradicts it.
  7. Stop the verifier in System, try a proof (it fails and the challenge stays unused), start it again and retry.
