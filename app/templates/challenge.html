What crosses, what stays

Crosses to AICTE
  • The bank's hostname and that its certificate is genuine
  • The request method and approved path
  • Status line, content type, date
  • The deposit-details response, whole
  • This deposit's record, nothing around it
Stays on your machine
  • Password, OTP, cookies, session headers
  • The query string
  • Your PAN, savings account and other deposits
  • Your local copy of the full response

Changes with the page you choose. After the proof you can compare both views byte for byte.
